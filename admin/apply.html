Welcome to YUMYUMPO

We're glad you're here. Before you fill out the application, here's what joining YUMYUMPO means right now.

Listing your restaurant is completely free. We're in early access — focused on building the best restaurant discovery experience in the Philippines, starting in El Nido. Every restaurant joining us during this phase becomes a founding partner.

As a founding partner, you get

  • •Your restaurant listed across Discover, Vibe Map, and Ask Fred AI search — free, with no commission and no transaction fees
  • •Founding partner recognition that carries forward when our Premium Membership launches
  • •Exclusive early-partner pricing on Premium features when they become available
  • •Your basic listing stays free for founding partners, even after Premium launches

What we're building toward

Premium Membership is coming — for restaurants that want to turn discovery into a full digital presence. When it launches, Premium partners will get:

  • •A dedicated restaurant website — designed and hosted by YUMYUMPO, branded to your restaurant, with your own link to share
  • •Order requests — let diners send order and reservation requests straight from your page
  • •Detailed analytics — see profile views, search appearances, follower growth, and what diners are looking for
  • •Promotions & announcements — push events, promos, and new menu items straight to your followers' feeds
  • •Priority placement — stronger visibility across Discover, the Vibe Map, and Ask Fred AI search
  • •A richer profile — full photo galleries, menu with photos, story, hours, and direct booking tools
  • •Priority support — direct help from the YUMYUMPO team

We'll announce pricing and timing well in advance — and founding partners always get first access and exclusive early-partner pricing.

What stays the same

We'll never charge you, enroll you in anything, or change your free listing without your explicit consent. That's a promise.

What happens next

Within 48 hours, our team will review your application. We'll look at your existing online presence — Google reviews, social media, any website you have — to get a feel for what makes your place special. If we have questions, we'll reach out directly.

If approved, you'll get an email with a sign-in link to your YUMYUMPO dashboard. From there, you can complete your profile, upload photos and menu, and customize how travelers see you.

Once your profile is live, your restaurant appears across Discover, Vibe Map, and Ask Fred AI — so travelers and locals exploring El Nido can find you immediately.

If you opted in for a hosted website, we'll send you a quick brief and start building. Most websites are live within 2 weeks of approval.

We'll reply to your email. Questions? Reach us anytime via the contact page.
